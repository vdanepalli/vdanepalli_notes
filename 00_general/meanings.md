# Meanings

- obscure -- conceal; uncertain; not discovered or known about
- mojibake -- garbled text that is the result of text being decoded using an unintended character encoding
- abstruse -- difficult to understand; obscure
- gobbledygook -- language that is meaningless or is made unitelligible by excessive use of abstruse technical terms; nonsense
- asinine -- extremely stupid or foolish
- rub -- a difficulty, especially one of central importance in a situation.
- abridge -- to reduce or lessen in duration, scope, authority etc...
- curtail -- to reduce or limit something, or to stop something before it is finished
- balk -- hesitate or be unwilling to accept an idea or undertaking
- sane -- sound mind, not made or mentally ill
