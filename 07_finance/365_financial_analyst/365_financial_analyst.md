- ACCOUNTING TYPES 
  - BOOKKEEPING -- gather
    - records every transaction/record/data

  - FINANCIAL ACCOUNTING
    - Output is several reports
    - Profitability, Revenue and Costs, Owns and Owes, Cash Balance
    - Bank, Investor -- use this 

    - ANNUAL REPORT 
      - INCOME STATEMENT (Profit and Loss)
        - how did company perform? Yearly, Quaterly
        - Revenue growth, Incidence of gross profit on revenues
        - REVENUE 
          - aka net sales -- inflow of economic resources
          - rent
          - sales
          - total revenue = net sales + other revenue
          - core vs non-core business activities
        - EXPENSES
          - outflow of economic resources
          - COGS -- Costs of Goods Sold
            - production cost
            - Gross Profit = Total Revenue - COGS
          - SG&A -- Selling, General and Administrative Expenses
            - Advertising and Promotions
            - Salaries
            - Office rent
            - Utility bills
          - D&A -- Depreciation and Amortization
            - the using up of tangible and intangible assets
            - DEPRECIATION
              - assets of physical nature
                - say you own a plant and every year its value decreases
                - to account for that we add a depreciation expense
            - AMORTIZATION
              - tangible 
                - goodwill
                - licenses
                - copyrights 
                - others
          - INTEREST EXPENSES
            - costs for receiving financing
          - TAXES
            - Corportate taxes
              - proportional to amount of pre-tax profit generated

        - company's financial position

        - liabilities owed

## Preparing a Cash Flow Statement

- Revenue != Cash
- Revenue on Income Statement != Cash received by the firm
- Recognizes revenue on realization and not on actual cash payment
- Revenues are identified when the product is transferred or the service is rendered
- Cash can be recieved before, at, or after (Kinda like pre-paid, cash on delivery, pay later) 
- ![](assets/2024-06-14-16-32-43.png)

<br/><br/>

- Revenue - Costs = Net Income != Cash
- Income != Cash
- Expenses are recorded when realized. Cash can be paid out before, at, or after

<br/><br/>

- Accrual Accounting
- Ensures that a firm records its economic operations in the period when they have been carried out
- Revenue is recorded when earned
- Costs are recorded when incurred
- Categories
  - Unearned Revenue
    - a payment has been recceived, while products/services have not been deliverede to clients yet
    - Is a liability because the money needs to be refunded in case of no delivery
  - Accrued Revenue
    - Products/Services have been delivered to clients but a payment has not been received yet
    - Is an asset
    - Revenue and Trade Receivables or Account Receivables
    - Revenue decreases and Trade Receivables increaes
    - Cash Inc  
  - Prepaid Expenses
    - Advancement payments for a future service
    - say office rent for next 6 months.
    - Cash decreases and Prepaid Expenses Increases
  - Accrued Expenses
    - Costs that have been incurred but haven't been paid yet
    - Accrued Expenses (say Personnel costs) != Trade Payables (Suppliers of goods and services)
    - is a liability
